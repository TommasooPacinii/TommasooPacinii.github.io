01Overview

A real landing page, built in real time
with an AI as co-designer.

ETS Wind is a renewable energy company specialized in the installation, maintenance and upgrade of next-generation wind turbines across 11 countries in Europe, the Middle East and North Africa. They operate at scale — over 2.6GW installed in 5 years.

The project was a complete marketing website built from scratch, using vibe coding — a collaborative approach where design decisions, UX structure, and frontend implementation were explored in real time through conversation with Claude AI.

02Challenge

Communicating industrial authority
without losing human clarity.

ETS Wind operates across three distinct audiences: technical operators on the field, C-suite stakeholders making investment decisions, and sustainability-focused partners looking for verified environmental impact. Each group enters with different expectations and reads the page differently.

The challenge was to design a single landing page that holds authority for all three — bold and industrial enough for technical credibility, clear and trustworthy enough for executive confidence, and mission-driven enough to communicate environmental purpose.
