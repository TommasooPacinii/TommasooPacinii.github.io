01Overview

A real landing page, built in real time
with an AI as co-designer.

ETS Wind is a renewable energy company specialised in the installation, maintenance and upgrade of next-generation wind turbines across 11 countries in Europe, the Middle East and North Africa. They operate at scale — over 2.6 GW installed in 5 years.

The project was a complete marketing website built from scratch using vibe coding — a collaborative approach where design decisions, UX structure and frontend implementation were explored in real time through conversation with Claude AI. No Figma handoff. No boilerplate. Just ideas and iteration.

02Challenge

Communicating industrial authority
without losing human clarity.

ETS Wind operates across three distinct audiences: technical operators on the field, C-suite stakeholders making investment decisions, and sustainability-focused partners. Each group enters with different expectations and reads the page differently.

The challenge was to design a single landing page that holds authority for all three — bold and industrial enough for technical credibility, clear and trustworthy for executive confidence, and mission-driven enough to communicate environmental purpose.

03Process

From a brief conversation
to a live product.

The entire workflow — from initial UX research to final CSS animations — was conducted through natural language conversation with Claude AI. Each section of the site was built interactively: describing intent, seeing a result, refining in real time.

The process revealed how much design thinking can happen in dialogue — and how fast a product moves when the conversation itself is the design tool.
