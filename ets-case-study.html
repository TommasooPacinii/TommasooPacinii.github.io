01 — UX / UI

Research & UX decisions.

Before pixels, research. I analysed reference portfolios and landing pages, defined the information architecture and built wireframes to lock in content hierarchy, grid and navigation flow.

02 — Figma prototyping

From wireframe to Figma mock-up.

In Figma I turned the wireframes into a high-fidelity mock-up: reusable components, auto-layout, typography and color styles. An interactive prototype to present and validate before writing code.

Components Auto-layout Interactive prototype
03 — Vibe Coding

Vibe coding
with AI.

From mock-up to a real site through natural language: I describe the intent, the AI generates the components, I iterate and refine. Less boilerplate, more time on design decisions.
